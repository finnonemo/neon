# Campaign Page content

Read before writing sections for a new or rebuilt Campaign Page (campaign, landing, event, recruiting or education page). This is content discipline only: Brand Intensity and the Brand Floor are unchanged. An edit to an existing page keeps its sections unless the employee asks to change them, and a Brand Intensity move keeps content, flow and composition, so neither re-derives sections from claims.

## Claims before sections

List what the page must convince a reader of as short claims, each with the proof the brief supplies: a fact, date, agenda item, link or supplied asset. Choose sections only after the list exists; each section carries at least one claim, and a claim with no supplied proof is stated plainly or dropped, never propped up with invented proof.

With a [Reference Design](../../neon-audit/references/reference-design.md), map the claims onto its section order. Drop or merge reference sections that carry no claim rather than filling them. Without one, as at Finnomena-led, order sections by what the reader needs first.

Put the claim list, with each claim's proof and section, in the Done report.

## First screen

At 375px and 1440px the first screen states what the page is and the next action, with that action visible without scrolling. For an event, the date and place appear there in large type, not only in a later details section.

## Headlines by rendered lines

Check each headline by its rendered line count at 375px and 1440px: two lines or fewer at both widths. Do not estimate from character counts; Thai has no spaces between words, so wrapping depends on the font, width and line-breaking. Count lines in a browser, with IBM Plex Sans Thai confirmed as the computed font. For example, take `getClientRects()` of each text node in the heading and count rects that overlap vertically as one line, so an inline glyph or span at another size does not add a line. If no browser can run, report the headline check as unverified; a character count is not evidence.

## No invented proof

Never invent testimonials, quotes, names, partner or customer logos, user or member counts, ratings, awards or returns. Use only what the brief or the employee supplies. Return and performance figures follow the [investment content](../../neon-audit/references/readiness.md#investment-content) rule.

When the request asks for such a section without supplying its content, fill it with clearly fictional sample content: sample names such as "ชื่อพนักงาน (ตัวอย่าง)", a quote that reads as an example, or labeled `[Partner logo]` slots. Do not add a demo banner. List each sample as a pre-launch to-do naming what replaces it. Under [readiness](../../neon-audit/references/readiness.md), sample content is a failed placeholder-copy item: a pre-launch to-do on a Ship-ready page, a Handoff Summary line on a Brand-ready one, and never itself a reason for Brand-ready.
