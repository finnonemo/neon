Title 2 Line and Title 2 Line Left hold `Title and Subtitle` (TEXT `Title`, `Subtitle`; `Show Subtitle`), Large Title holds them under `Controls`. Every title is one line, truncated at the end. Heights with Search off: 58, Large Title 133. `Toolbar - Top`: padding 8 · 0 · 8 · 0 (`spacing-03`), so 64, Large Title 139 (115 without Subtitle), Search on 120. `Controls` hugs with min height `control-medium` (48), every title is in its flow and hugs its height, and Leading and Trailing fill around Default's and Title 2 Line's title, so it stays centred; Default's title box hugs up to 258. Large Title 146 (122 without Subtitle).

Top - iPad: in Title 2 Line, Title 2 Line Left and Compact Large, Leading and Trailing hug, the title sits in the row between them, Fill, and the row's gap is 12 (`spacing-04`). Title 2 Line centres its title in the room left; the others start it at the leading end. 58 tall as before.

Window controls: every `Toolbar - Top - iPad` variant's `Leading` Slot starts with a hidden `Frame` (51 × 22 shown, padding 10 · 0) holding `Window Controls`, an instance of the kit's `_Window Controls` (`Window State=Active`). A windowed iPad app shows that Frame on the leftmost bar's instance, as Screen Template's iPad Window does; an overlay of the kit's controls doubles them (pawisssh/design-system#630). In a script, find it by that layer name, `Window Controls`, not the kit's `_Window Controls`.

Layer order (2026-11-08): an absolute title is the bottom layer (in `Toolbar - Top`, an in-flow title before `Trailing`, which stays the last child of `Controls`; likewise in Top - iPad's Title 2 Line, Title 2 Line Left and Compact Large), and a vertical variant whose first child holds `Trailing` (`Controls`) stacks first on top (`itemReverseZIndex`), so an open [Menu](menu.md) (`Expand=True`) in `Trailing` draws over the title, Tab Bar and Search.

## Usage

Each set has its own Usage row. Top - iPhone's Usage holds the kit look's `Samples`. Toolbar - Top's Usage holds `Samples`, which took over Top navigation bar's samples: Default, Large Title with Subtitle, `Show Search`, long titles, a Text button in Trailing, a content bar (`Show Title` off, Tertiary Button with a chevron in Leading), Focus, and a 1440 Regular width `Screen` with Breadcrumbs under the bar. Web and Android use Toolbar - Top filled to the screen's width.

Top - iPad in a narrow column: the bar keeps its Style; its Slots hold less, as the kit's narrow bars do. The Search field becomes an Icon button; the window controls stay where the screen shows them. Top - iPad's Usage shows both cases in `Narrow column`.

Top - iPhone Duo on the side (Apple's [Designing for iPhone Duo](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/designing-for-iphone-duo), read 2026-11-11). Closed, and open in landscape, the bar's items move into the `Vertical Bar` on the right edge with the status bar and Navigation's Rail; open in portrait keeps horizontal bars (Screen templates). On that bar:

- **Order**: from the top, navigation (back, close), then the prominent action (Done), then the other items in their groups. A gap keeps the top bar's items apart from the bottom bar's. Group items rather than spacing them by hand.
- **Overflow**: when the bar runs out of room, items go into the overflow menu from the bottom up. A visibility priority changes that order, whole groups first, then single items. Keep frequent actions (Compose, New Note) and badged items visible longest. Use the system's overflow menu; the ellipsis is for it alone, so another menu takes a different symbol.
- **Titles**: every item has a title as well as a symbol, even where only the symbol shows, since the overflow menu lists it by title. A text-only button stays in a horizontal bar, so prefer a symbol.
- Which bar gives way first, the toolbar or the tab bar: [navigation.md](navigation.md).

- **Placement**: the Toolbar - Top family (`Toolbar - Top`, `- iPhone`, `- iPad`, `- iPhone Duo`) is docked to the top edge of its column, directly below the status bar (iOS and Android; web has none), full column width; Content's and Inspector's each have their own. It collapses as the page scrolls (a Large title becomes the standard title) and the screen draws the scroll-edge fade. Over a map or a photo it floats: leading control and up to two trailing, no title, with background protection behind the icons. A Snackbar sits over it. `Toolbar - Bottom - iPhone` sits on the bottom edge of the view.
