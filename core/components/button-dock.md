# Button dock

> Core's Button dock, compiled for the web: the `iOS=False` look at the Large text size. Generated by the Core sync; do not edit.

## Anatomy and layer tree

One public set; no `_` Item (each button is a nested `Button`). Layer names follow Base's.

```
Button dock (variant root)          Fill × Hug, no fill, no padding, VERTICAL
   │                                stretched left-right; visible on `Overflow`
   │                                constraints stretch it to the Container on both axes; visible on `Overflow`
   ├─ Top spacing                   frame, Fill × Fixed 16 (Container/<Default · Split>/Padding top), no fill; visible on `Top spacing`
   ├─ Accessory                     SLOT, Fill × Hug, padding bottom 8 (Container/*/Gap vertical); visible on `Show Accessory`
   │  └─ Checkbox                   default content: Type=Inset, label "I agree to the terms and conditions"
   ├─ Buttons                       Fill × Hug, no padding; VERTICAL, gap 8 (Stacked, Two, One) ·
   │  │                             HORIZONTAL, space between, gap 16 (Split)
   │  ├─ Leading                    hidden by default, shown by `Button 3` (Stacked, Split); VERTICAL Fill (Stacked) · HORIZONTAL Hug (Split)
   │  │  ├─ Button 4                Button, Secondary, Large, exposed; visible on `Button 4`
   │  │  └─ Button 3                Button, Secondary, Large, exposed
   │  └─ Trailing                   HORIZONTAL (Two, Three, Split; gap 8 · 8 · 16) · VERTICAL (Stacked, One; gap 8)
   │     ├─ Secondary               Button, Hierarchy=Tertiary (BD1; Secondary on Three), Size=Large, exposed; hidden on One
   │     └─ Primary                 Button, Hierarchy=Primary (Secondary on Three), Size=Large, exposed; first on Stacked
   │                                Buttons Fill on Stacked, Two, Three, One; Hug on Split
```

- **Root carries nothing**: the surface fill, padding and gap sit on `Container` (structure.md).
- **Button, not Rect button.** The throwaway nests `Rect button`, which is the square-cornered set; `Button` is the Capsule (ADR 0008) and carries the `iOS` the dock pins. `Size=Large` (56), not `Large (Expressive)`, which fixes the width at 256 ([button.md](button.md)). The Button root is Hug × Hug; the dock sets each instance's width to Fill.
- **Order.** Side by side, Secondary is first (left) and Primary last (right): Carbon's modal footer, the kit's example and v2's XR. Stacked, Primary is on top: Carbon and Base.
- **Exposed Buttons.** Both are exposed instances, so their own `Label Text`, icons and `State` show on the dock, as Toggletip exposes its footer Button ([toggletip.md](toggletip.md)). The dock has no TEXT property of its own.
- **The hairline is a layer**, so `Overflow` can bind its visibility (a BOOLEAN binds `visible`, not a stroke). Carbon's Toolbar - iOS draws the same line as a top stroke; the token is the same.
- **Kit parts** sit unchanged on the kit's variables. The home indicator is an exposed instance, so its own `Show Home Indicator` is the designer's toggle and the dock needs no BOOLEAN of its own (call 5) (reversed: not exposed, since its only properties are Device and Orientation; BOOLEAN `Home indicator` instead).
- **Accessory** is a Slot (`Show Accessory` beside it, per the Slot naming rule). Its content isn't pinned; Checkbox has no `iOS` axis, so nothing goes stale between looks.

## Axes

| Axis | Values (default first) | Drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Layout` | Stacked · Two · Three · One · Split (call 1) | Stacked: Primary over the bottom button, both full width, the default and the AX-size form of Two and Three; Two: the bottom button and Primary side by side, equal halves; Three: three equal related actions side by side, a third each, all Secondary, no Primary (H7); One: Primary alone; Split: wider than 600, Secondary and Primary at the trailing end and Button 3 · 4 at the leading end, each as wide as its label |

Properties (as built):
- `Top spacing` (BOOLEAN, default True): the 16 above the dock content.
- `Show Accessory` (BOOLEAN, default False) and the `Accessory` SLOT.
- `Button 3` and `Button 4` (BOOLEAN, default False; Stacked and Split): up to two more Secondary actions; `Button 4` shows with `Button 3`.
- `Home indicator` (BOOLEAN, default True).
- The exposed Buttons' own properties (`Label Text`, icons, `State`).

5 × 2 = **10 variants**, 17 variables in `Components` (`Button dock/Container/<Default · Split>` × 6 each, `Actions/<Default · Split>` × 2 each, `Line/Default` × 1). The set's default variant is `Layout=Stacked, iOS=False`.

## Icon records

None. The buttons bring their own icon records; the dock draws no glyph.

## Text styles, wrapping and truncation

The dock draws no text of its own and has no TEXT property: each nested part keeps its own.

| Layer | Text style | Lines | Text property |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Primary` › label | Button's (`Size=Large`) | one line, never cut (Button's rule) | the exposed Button's `Label Text` |
| `Secondary` › label | Button's (`Size=Large`) | one line, never cut | the exposed Button's `Label Text` |
| `Middle` › label (`Three`) | Button's (`Size=Large`) | one line, never cut | the exposed Button's `Label Text` |
| `Accessory` › Checkbox label | Checkbox's | wraps (Checkbox's rule) | the Checkbox's own, in the Slot |

Side by side, each button is half the width less 4: about 167 on a 375 screen. A Thai label that doesn't fit on one line there is the case for `Layout=Stacked` (call 1); a label is never cut. On `Three` each is a third less the gaps: 109 at 343, where "สับเปลี่ยน" is 63 wide; a label that won't fit is the case for `Stacked`.

## Hit target and Skeleton

- **Hit target**: None of its own. Each Button brings its 56 target on the Root (ADR 0003); the Container's 16 padding keeps them clear of the edge.
- **Skeleton**: None (assumption). The dock's actions are known when the screen is drawn; a button that waits on data shows its own `State=Loading` or `Disabled`. A designer who needs one sets the nested Button's `State=Skeleton`.

## Motion

None (pawisssh/design-system#161). The dock is pinned and doesn't move; the scroll edge effect is the system's own and the buttons keep their press motion.

## Usage

Built 2026-11-06 in the `Usage` frame on `Button docks`: Light and Dark columns, each with these samples and a caption under each.

- `Stacked`, the default: "ยืนยันการโอนเงิน" (Primary) over "ยกเลิก" (Tertiary, BD1). Cancel sits at the bottom; with no Cancel, Primary is nearest the thumb. It is the layout at AX text sizes, or whenever a label might not fit half the width in every language (R2).
- `Two` (`iOS=False`): "ยกเลิก" (Tertiary) · "ยืนยัน", only when both labels fit half the width in every language.
- `One`: "ดำเนินการต่อ", with `Overflow`: the hairline (`iOS=False`) or the scroll edge effect shows only while content scrolls under the dock.
- `Top spacing` and `Overflow` off (`Three`, `iOS=False`): "ซื้อ" · "ขาย" · "สับเปลี่ยน", three equal related actions, all Secondary, no Primary (H7), for content above that already leaves room (R5).
- `Show Accessory`: Checkbox "ฉันยอมรับข้อกำหนดและเงื่อนไข" over "เปิดบัญชี".
- Four actions on `Stacked` (`iOS=False`): Button 4 "ลบรายการ" (Danger, at the top, farthest from the thumb), Button 3 "บันทึกแบบร่าง", Primary "ส่งคำขอ", "ยกเลิก". Four at most, all Large (R3).
- Focus, once, on Primary (`Two`, `iOS=False`): the ring is the Button's own.
- `Split` at 1024 (`iOS=False`), for screens wider than 600 (R4): "บันทึกแบบร่าง" at the leading end, "ยกเลิก" · "ส่งคำขอ" at the trailing end, each as wide as its label.
- Don'ts: more than four buttons or mixed sizes; side by side when a label might not fit half (Two) or a third (Three) of the width; Cancel on top of a stack; `Split` at 600 or narrower; Dialog or Sheet actions, or toolbar icons (they carry their own buttons).
- Where it sits: pinned to a screen's bottom edge, full width. The Pattern or screen sets its max width and its placement on a wide screen; the set has no width axis (ADR 0013).
- What not to use it for, as first proposed: "more than two actions (a Menu or a second screen; Base's Button 3–4 and v2 Compact's Leading pair aren't carried over, assumption)" (reversed: Button 3 and Button 4, four at most).

- **Placement**: the bottom edge, sticky, with the Home indicator inside it. A screen with a Button dock has no tab bar (a pushed form): the dock is the bottom edge's only bar (R1). On the keyboard it rides up and sits on top of it (R2).

### Hierarchy

Which `Hierarchy` and `Type` its nested buttons take, and why. The rules (1–6) and the owner's calls (H1–H7) are in [hierarchy.md](../hierarchy.md); Button's own values are in [button.md › Hierarchy](button.md). Source: Uber Base [Button dock, Usage](https://base.uber.com/6d2425e9f/p/46ed03-button-dock/b/46994b), read 2026-11-09. "Agrees" checks the spec's pin; the set is built from the spec.

| Slot | Pin | Rule | Agrees |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Trailing/Primary` | Button `Primary · Default · Large`; `Secondary` on `Three` | rule 1: the screen's one main action, at the bottom edge; on `Three`, [H7](../hierarchy.md): equal related actions, no Primary | yes |
| `Trailing/Secondary` (the bottom button) | Button `Tertiary · Default · Large` (BD1); `Secondary` on `Three` | rule 4: the bottom button is the way out, Tertiary as in Uber's dock; on `Three`, H7 | yes |
| `Trailing/Middle` (`Three` only) | Button `Secondary · Default · Large` | H7: the middle one of three equal related actions | yes |
| `Leading/Button 3`, `Leading/Button 4` | Button `Secondary · Default · Large` | rule 3, H4: supporting actions above Primary, as Uber stacks its Secondaries | yes |

- **Pairs.** The Opposite pair (Primary + Tertiary, [button.md › Pairs](button.md)) is the default: the bottom button is pinned Tertiary (BD1). The Related pair (Primary + Secondary, H4) is Primary with `Button 3` above it. `Three` holds no pair: three equal Secondaries and no Primary ([H7](../hierarchy.md)).
- Every dock button is `Large`, side by side or stacked (rule 2). `Type=Danger` goes on `Button 4` at the top (rule 5), as Uber puts destructive actions farthest from the thumb.
- The Usage samples put "ยกเลิก" (Cancel) on the bottom button, Tertiary since BD1, in `Stacked`, `Two`, the four-action sample and `Split`. The `Top spacing` sample is `Three`: "ซื้อ" · "ขาย" · "สับเปลี่ยน", all Secondary (H7).
