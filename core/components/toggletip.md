# Toggletip

> Core's Toggletip, compiled for the web: the `iOS=False` look at the Large text size. Generated by the Core sync; do not edit.

## Anatomy and layer tree

The public `Toggletip` (trigger plus overflowing tip) is in ; `_Toggletip definition` in R5. The tip, `_Toggletip item`, is Carbon's construction (Q11, owner 2026-11-05), as in Carbon Native Mobile's `_Tooltip item`:

```
_Toggletip item (variant root)      Fill × Hug in the set (288 cells), no fill, no padding (Q6)
└─ Container                        VERTICAL, Fill × Hug, min width 288, gap −1; cross-axis align by Alignment (MIN · CENTER · MAX);
   │                                itemReverseZIndex true at Position=Bottom, false at Top
   ├─ Caret                         Hug × Hug, padding 0 · 16; first at Position=Bottom, last at Top (itemIndex)
   │  └─ Pointer                    instance of `_Toggletip caret` (Direction Up at Bottom, Down at Top)
   └─ Surface                       VERTICAL, Fill × Hug, padding 16, gap 16, radius 12, layer-01, 1 border-subtle inside, the one shadow
      ├─ Content (SLOT)             VERTICAL, Fill × Hug, gap 4; default content below, no properties
      │  ├─ Title                   Headings/Headline, Fill, HEIGHT
      │  └─ Description             Body/Subheadline, Fill, HEIGHT
      └─ Footer                     instance of `_Toggletip actions`, Action=Tertiary + Primary, Fill, exposed; BOOLEAN `Footer` (on)

_Toggletip actions (part)           Action Primary · Tertiary + Primary · Tertiary
└─ Container                        HORIZONTAL, Fill × Hug, padding 0 16, gap 16, cross-axis centre
   ├─ Left                          HORIZONTAL, Fill × Hug, start · centre; hidden at Action=Primary
   │  └─ Tertiary                   Link, Highlight Enabled Large, Hug, no trailing icon, exposed, "Link"
   ├─ Primary                       Button, Primary Default, Size Medium, Fill, no icons, exposed, "Got it"
   └─ Spacer                        Fill × 1, no fill; shown when one action is, so it keeps the start half

_Toggletip caret (part)             Direction Up · Down
└─ Pointer                          16 × 8 frame that clips
   └─ Shape                         POLYGON, 3 points, layer-01 fill, 1 border-subtle stroke inside
```

- **One caret.** Position moves it (`itemIndex` 0 at Bottom, 1 at Top) and turns it (`Direction` Up · Down on the `_Toggletip caret` part, Carbon's `_Tooltip caret item`). Alignment is Container's `counterAxisAlignItems`: the Caret hugs, so Start and End put the pointer 16 in, and the shape needs no per-variant `x`.
- **Caret over the border.** The Caret overlaps the Surface by 1 (gap −1) and draws above it, so its fill covers the Surface's border where they meet. Container draws its first child on top at Bottom and its last at Top: the engine's `itemReverseZIndex` literal rule (added for this, spec-format.md). A 3-point POLYGON puts its corners on the box's inscribed ellipse, so `Shape` is 20.78 × 12: the triangle is 16 wide where the Pointer cuts it at 8, and the clip hides the shape's own base stroke. Checked by eye at 6× at Bottom and Top, Light and Dark (2026-11-05).
- **Content** is a SLOT (figma-instances.md), added after the build. Its default content is the Title and Description. A Pattern replaces them with a Link, Progress steps or other text. The Port sample on the bases frame puts a Title over a Progress steps sample. Figma refuses a property link on a layer inside a Slot, so Title and Description have no properties: designers edit or hide them in the slot (owner, 2026-11-05: "Keep Slot, drop 3 props").
- Its `Action` is Carbon's `Type` (Primary · Ghost+Primary · Ghost) in Core's names: Carbon's Ghost is Button's `Hierarchy=Tertiary`, and the axis is `Action` because Core's `Type` is a colour role (GLOSSARY), as Contextual banner's `Action` is. Each action takes half the row; with one, a `Spacer` holds the end half, so the lone one sits at the start as Carbon's does. The Tertiary is a Link since the owner's swap (below): a Fill `Left` frame takes the half and the Link hugs at its start. Carbon's Large (48) is Core's Medium, kept over Small (36) by the owner (2026-11-05: "Keep Medium"). The part and its Buttons are exposed, so a Toggletip instance shows `Action` and both labels. On by default; turn it off for a tip with no action. A tip with a Footer is 157 against 93 without.
- **Footer reversed** (owner, 2026-11-05: "Toggletip footer → _Actions"): the Link + Button row (Link Medium at the start, Button XSmall Primary at the end, SPACE_BETWEEN) is replaced by the part. The footer Link is now a Tertiary Button; a Link still goes in the Slot (reversed by the owner, 2026-11-05: the Tertiary is a Link again, `Link` Highlight · Large in a Fill `Left` frame, with 16 at the Container's start).
- **Not in the set**: the anchor (Q3), a close ✕ (Q10), and ~~a footer Button~~ or pagination (the map: never a form, menu or navigation) (reversed by the owner 2026-11-05: Footer Link + Button, on by default; then `_Toggletip actions`, the same day).

## Axes

`Type` Standard · Definition · Icon (the trigger) × `Position` Bottom · Top × `Alignment` Center · Start · End × `Visible` False · True = **36 variants**, no properties of its own (the exposed `Item` shows `Content` and `Footer`, the exposed triggers their icon or `Term`). `_Toggletip definition`: `State` Enabled · Active · Focus, TEXT `Term`. `_Toggletip item`:

| Axis | Values (default first) | Drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Position` | Bottom · Top | Where the tip sits from its anchor (Carbon's sense). Bottom puts the Caret first, pointing up; Top puts it last, pointing down (Q4) |
| `Alignment` | Center · Start · End | The Caret's place across Container: centred, or 16 in from the start or end (Q5) |

Properties: SLOT `Content`; BOOLEAN `Footer` (on). The proposal's BOOLEAN `Title` and TEXT `Title text` and `Description text` are dropped: Figma can't link them inside the Slot (owner, 2026-11-05: "Keep Slot, drop 3 props"). 2 × 3 = **6 variants**.

## Hit target and Skeleton

- **Hit target**: None. The tip itself isn't a control: a tap inside it does nothing, and a Link in its Content brings its own target (inline links are exempt, Layering and structure). The anchor's target is the anchor's (Q3).
- **Skeleton**: None. It is an overlay (Variant axes): it opens on a tap with its content ready.

## Usage

- **Use it** to explain one thing beside it: a term ("ราคา NAV"), a rule ("ยกเลิกคำสั่งได้ก่อน 11.00 น."), or a short status list (Port's order steps). One at a time.
- **Don't** put a form, a menu, navigation or a choice in it (a Menu or Dropdown menu, a Sheet). Don't use it for a page's main information, which belongs on the page, or for feedback after an action, which a Snackbar gives. Don't open it on hover or on its own: Tooltip is Out, and Base's onboarding popovers aren't Core.
- **The anchor** is the host's: the public `Toggletip` carries its trigger and goes where the anchor was; the tip meets the trigger's 48 box, 12 below the glyph). it meets the trigger's 48 box, 12 from an Icon button's glyph; R7), and its caret points at the anchor's centre. At Start or End the caret's centre is 24 in, the centre of a 48 Icon button at the edge.
- **Placement**: Bottom by default (Carbon on mobile). Top when there's no room below, as with Port's status row near the bottom of a Tile. Pick the Alignment that keeps the tip 16 from the screen's edges. Center keeps it centred on the anchor (Base's default).
- **Footer**: on when the tip leads to an action (a Link to more, a Button that acts or dismisses); off for a tip that only explains. What the Button does and the focus order with it are open (see Open).
- **A term explained**: a "ราคา NAV" row with an ⓘ anchor and a Bottom · Start tip with the Footer on. **Above the anchor**: Top · Center, Footer off. **Footer actions**: `Action` Tertiary + Primary, Primary alone and Tertiary alone. **Order status**: Top · Start with Progress steps in the Slot. The **Don'ts** are listed under them. Each tip's caret sits on its anchor's centre. the Footer actions stack is three as well (Standard · Bottom · Start, Visible True), spaced so each tip clears the next trigger.
