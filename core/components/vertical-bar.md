# Vertical Bar

> Core's Vertical Bar, compiled for the web: the `iOS=False` look at the Large text size. Generated by the Core sync; do not edit.

## Anatomy and layer tree

```
Vertical Bar (variant root)        Fixed 84 × Fill, no fill, no padding
├─ Camera Punchout                 Fixed 48 × 42, at the top or the bottom (`Camera`), hidden for None
├─ Top                             VERTICAL, gap 12, padding 24 / 24 / 0 / 12 (top 74 under a camera at the top), Fill × Hug
│  ├─ Status bar - iPhone Duo      kit part, 48 × 83; `Show Status Bar`
│  ├─ Overflow                     the same Icon button, the ellipsis (Toolbar compressed)
│  └─ Toolbar items                Slot, Share + a two-button Icon button group by default (Tab bar compressed)
└─ Navigation                      instance, `Layout=Rail`, `Items=5`, `Type=Search Role`, `Minimized` pinned by `Compression`; absolute, Stretch × Bottom; `Show Navigation`
```

The Camera Punchout sits where the outer camera is: bottom in Outer Landscape, top in Outer Portrait, absent on the inner display (its camera is hidden until used). The Rail's bar is Navigation's own 48 × 270, or 48 × 108 at `Minimized=True` ([navigation.md](navigation.md)), at x 12 of the strip; this component places it, it does not redraw it. With the camera at the bottom the Rail ends 74 above the strip's foot (24 otherwise), and Back and the status bar are off, since 466 has no room for them beside the Rail (Build log).

## Axes

| Axis | Values (default first) | Drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Compression` | Toolbar compressed · Tab bar compressed | Toolbar compressed: `Overflow` shows, the Slot is hidden, Navigation `Minimized=False`. Tab bar compressed: the Slot shows, `Overflow` is hidden, Navigation `Minimized=True` |
| `Camera` | Bottom · Top · None | Where the `Camera Punchout` sits: Bottom (Outer Landscape), Top (Outer Portrait), None (Inner Landscape) |

Properties: BOOLEAN `Show Back` (on, the owner's call), `Show Status Bar` (on), `Show Navigation` (on; off where a pose draws the floating Sidebar instead of the Rail), SLOT `Toolbar items`. 2 × 3 = **6 variants**.

A BOOLEAN's default is set-wide, so `Show Back` and `Show Status Bar` are linked in the four Top and None variants only. In the two `Camera=Bottom` variants both layers are hidden and unlinked: the owner chose that over the 10 px overlap of Overflow and the Rail at 466 ("Confirm, hide Back"), and the kit hides the status bar there. `Show Navigation` is linked in all six.

The strip's height is Fill: each variant is drawn at its pose's `Display` height (Bottom 466, Top 678, None 669), and a template stretches its instance to `Display`'s.

## Component variables and Theme tokens

`Components`: none of its own. The strip's 84 width, the 12 inset, the 8 gap and the Camera Punchout's 48 × 42 are raw, as the Rail's 24 padding is ([navigation.md](navigation.md)); they are the kit's, not Scale steps. `Theme`: none; the strip has no fill, and its parts bind the tokens they already have.

## Usage

- Use Toolbar compressed (the default) for navigation-focused views: the tab bar and primary destinations stay, the toolbar goes into `…`. Use Tab bar compressed for task-focused views, where the toolbar's actions are the task: the tab bar becomes one control.
- Back is first and Share, or the prominent action, next, as the HIG orders them. Reserve `…` for overflow.
- The templates use it in all three rail poses: Outer Landscape, Outer Portrait, Inner Landscape. Inner Portrait keeps its horizontal bars.
- Not for Split View's left edge: the strip keeps its right-edge drawing (Screen templates).
