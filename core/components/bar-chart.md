# Bar chart

> Core's Bar chart, compiled for the web: the `iOS=False` look at the Large text size. Generated by Core's Web compile; do not edit.

## Anatomy and layer tree

```
Bar chart (variant root)            Fill × Hug, no fill, no padding
└─ Container                        VERTICAL, gap Container/<Size>/Gap vertical (chart to legend), Fill × Hug
   ├─ Chart                         HORIZONTAL, gap Content/<Size>/Gap horizontal (8), Fill × Hug
   │  ├─ Y axis                     VERTICAL, space between, Hug × Fixed (the plot's height); three labels, right-aligned, $caption1, Text/text-secondary; shown by `Y axis`
   │  └─ Plot column                VERTICAL, gap Content/<Size>/Gap vertical (4), Fill × Hug
   │     ├─ Plot                    FRAME, Fill × Fixed 160 · 96 (Content/<Size>/Plot height)
   │     │  ├─ Gridlines            ABSOLUTE; top, middle and base: a gridline is a LINE, 2 dotted `0 4` round, chart-gridline; on Gain/loss the middle one is the zero line, a solid 1 FRAME in chart-axis, and on the others the base is
   │     │  ├─ Bars                 SLOT, HORIZONTAL, gap Item/<Size>/Gap horizontal, Fill × Fill; `Column 1` … `Column 6`, each a `_Bar column`
   │     │  └─ Empty text           centred, $subhead, Text/text-secondary; shown when State=Empty
   │     └─ X axis                  HORIZONTAL, gap Item/<Size>/Gap horizontal, Fill × Hug; one label per column, FILL, centred, $caption1, Text/text-secondary
   └─ Legend                        HORIZONTAL wrap, gap Legend/<Size>/Gap horizontal × Gap vertical (16 × 8), Fill × Hug; `Legend item` instances; shown when Legend=Inline

_Bar column                         VERTICAL, Fill × Fill, gap 0: `Above` (Fill × Fill, bottom-aligned) over `Below` (Fill × Fill, top-aligned, shown on Gain/loss)
├─ Above                            `Bar` (Simple, a gain) or `Segment 3`, `Segment 2`, `Segment 1` (Stacked, top first)
└─ Below                            `Loss`
```

- **The X axis sits under the plot inside `Plot column`**, Carbon's grouping, so its labels line up with the bars whether the `Y axis` shows or not.
- **Bars FILL and the gap is fixed** (Q11), so 6 or 12 columns share the plot's width the same way, and each X label, also FILL in a row with the same gap, sits under its column. CDS's `barPadding = 0.1` is a ratio of the band; a fixed gap is the nearest bindable form.
- **The radius is on the end away from zero** (Q10): `Bar` and `Segment 3` round their top corners, `Loss` its bottom corners, and the inner segments none. This is CDS's default (`roundBaseline` off). The pill (`borderRadius={1000}`, CDS's `stacked (rounded)`) is left out. A rule's `cornerRadius` binds all four corners, so the per-corner 4 is a literal on the base, carried into every variant.
- **The columns are plain frames in this set**, as the Line chart's `_Line series` and the Allocation bar's `_Legend item` were. A sample sets its own heights and colours by script; an Item set of bar parts comes later, if hosts need one.
- **The bar heights are illustrative**, as the Line chart's path is. The variant draws six columns at fixed heights that read true for the type; a sample sets its own heights by script, and code draws bars from data.
- The Y axis has three labels, the top, middle and base gridlines' values (on Gain/loss: a gain, `0`, a loss). It has no title: the host's header names the unit.

## Axes

| Axis | Values (default first) | Drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Type` | Simple · Stacked · Gain/loss | Simple: one `Bar` per column, `Categorical/Full/01`. Stacked: three segments per column, `Categorical/Full/01` to `03` from the base up. Gain/loss: a `Bar` in `chart-positive` above the zero line, or a `Loss` in `chart-negative` below it (columns 3 and 5 in the variant); the zero line moves to the middle |
| `State` | Data · Empty · Skeleton | Data: the bars. Empty: the gridlines and the `Empty text`, no bars. Skeleton: the plot in `skeleton-background`, no gridlines or labels, the same height |
| `Size` | Medium · Small | the plot's height (160 · 96), the gaps and label row |
| `Legend` | None · Inline | whether the legend shows. Inline for Stacked; None where one series or the colour's meaning (gain and loss) is named by the host |

Properties: `Y axis` (BOOLEAN, default off: Q9), `Bars` (SLOT), `Empty text` (TEXT, English default), `X label 1` … `X label 6` (TEXT), `Y label top`, `Y label middle`, `Y label base` (TEXT). 3 × 3 × 2 × 2 = **36 variants** on the chart, and no nested set.

## Component variables and Theme tokens

`Components`, Tag's three-level shape, in the Extension's local collection. The groups are Core's closed list, so the plot is `Content` and the bar `Item`, as in the Line chart.

| Group | Keyed by | Properties | Values (Medium · Small) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Bar chart/Container` | `Size` | Gap vertical (chart to legend) | `spacing-04` (12) · `spacing-03` (8) |
| `Bar chart/Content` | `Size` | Plot height, Gap horizontal (Y axis to plot), Gap vertical (plot to X axis) | `spacing-13` (160) · `spacing-12` (96); `spacing-03` (8) · `spacing-03` (8); `spacing-02` (4) · `spacing-02` (4) |
| `Bar chart/Item` | `Size` | Gap horizontal (between columns) | `spacing-03` (8) · `spacing-02` (4) |
| `Bar chart/Legend` | `Size` | Gap horizontal, Gap vertical | `spacing-05` (16), `spacing-03` (8) at both |
| `Bar chart/Label` | role: `Axis`, `Empty text` | Type token, Type emphasis, Align horizontal, Align vertical, Decoration | `$caption1` Regular · `$subhead` Regular; `Center`, `Top`, `-` |

`Legend` joins lint's group list for this set: the Allocation bar put its legend gaps in `Content`, which here holds the plot's.

Templates: `Components/Bar chart/<Group>/{{Size=*}}/<Property>`. **Literals**: the zero-line height (1), the gridlines' `0 4` dash and round cap and the Gridlines frame's 1 inset, as in the Line chart; the per-corner radius 4 (Theme's `Radius/4px` step, which a rule can't bind to two corners) and its 60% smoothing, set on the base; the illustrative bar heights. The bar minimum (1) is code's, not a Figma value.

`Theme`: no Component-scoped token.

| Theme token | Light | Dark | Paired with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Categorical/Full/01` … `08` (Extension collection) | Carbon Categorical, Option 1 | Option modes | 3:1 on `Layer/layer-01`: Light 3.16–8.09, Dark 3.01–6.05 |
| `Chart/chart-positive` | Green/135 | Green/115 | **3.85** Light, **7.50** Dark |
| `Chart/chart-negative` | Red/125 | Red/115 | **5.39** Light, **4.02** Dark |
| `Chart/chart-gridline`, `Chart/chart-axis` | Navy/12A · Navy/25A | White/15A · White/25A | decorative |
| `Text/text-secondary`, `Text/text-primary` | as Core | as Core | labels on the host surface; the legend's label is `text-primary` |
| `Skeleton/skeleton-background` | as Tile | as Tile | decorative |

Stacked segments touch with no gap, so two adjacent Categorical colours must hold Carbon's own order; the Extension's order 01 → 08 is the one checked for adjacent contrast.

## Icon records

`Legend item`'s `square-small` swatch (Font Awesome Sharp Solid, `Allocation bar/Icon/Small`), reused as built. The chart draws no other Icon.

## Text styles, wrapping and truncation

| Layer | Text style | Lines | Text property |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X labels | `$caption1` (12, the Thai floor for one line) | one line, centred under its column, never cut | `X label 1` … `6` |
| Y labels | `$caption1` | one line, right-aligned | `Y label top`, `middle`, `base` |
| `Empty text` | `$subhead` (15) | up to two lines, centred | `Empty text` |
| `Legend item` label | `$subhead` (15/24) | one line | `Label text` (property `Label`) |

The host picks X labels short enough for a column (a month `ม.ค.`, a quarter `Q1`). Twelve columns at 343 leave about 21 each at Medium: a three-character Thai month fits; a longer label means fewer labels, not a smaller style.

## Hit target and Skeleton

- **Hit target**: none. The chart doesn't take a touch in this version: no bar is selectable and no tooltip shows (see Calls). A host that needs a tap wraps the chart.
- **Skeleton**: `State=Skeleton` fills the plot with `skeleton-background` at the same height, with no gridlines, labels or legend, so nothing shifts on load.

## Usage

- Both modes, Light and its Dark twin, with Thai copy.
- **A monthly value**: Simple, six months `ม.ค.` … `มิ.ย.`, `Y axis` on.
- **A split per month**: Stacked, three series, `Legend=Inline`.
- **A cash flow**: Gain/loss, six months with two losses, the zero line in the middle.
- **No data** and **Loading**: Empty with `ไม่มีข้อมูลในช่วงนี้`, then Skeleton.
- **A card**: `Size=Small`, Simple, `Y axis` off.
- Use it to compare values across a few categories or periods. Don't use it for a split of one whole ([Allocation bar](allocation-bar.md)) or a price over time ([Line chart](line-chart.md)).
