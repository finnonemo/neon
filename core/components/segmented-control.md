# Segmented control

> Core's Segmented control, compiled for the web: the `iOS=False` look at the Large text size. Generated by the Core sync; do not edit.

## Anatomy and layer tree

Two components. The public set is a track holding a Slot, and the private `_Segmented control item` is one segment. Layer names follow Carbon's.

```
Segmented control (variant root)     Fill × Hug, no fill, no padding; min height Interactive/<Size>, Container centred
└─ Container                         HORIZONTAL, Fill × Hug, min height Segmented control/Container/<Size>/Layout min-height,
   └─ Segments                       SLOT, HORIZONTAL, Fill × Fill, gap 0, Items centred vertically (T2): _Segmented control item instances, each Fill × Hug
                                     (hidden on Skeleton)

```

- **Equal widths by layout**: `Segments` is Fill and every item is Fill wide, so the widths are equal and come from the track, not from the labels. The item's side padding is only the floor where the labels nearly fill a segment (call 9).
- **Selected indicator**: in Figma it is the selected item's own fill. The slide between segments happens in code (call 12, Motion).
- The Slot defaults to three items: the first is `Selected=True`, and the labels are `Option 1`, `Option 2` and `Option 3` (English defaults, owner 2026-11-04).
- `Segments` is a Slot (`Segments#1907:0`, seen 2026-11-05), made by hand after the first build. Since pawisssh/design-system#357 the spec lists it (SLOT `Segments`), so `build` links it and `verify` checks it (12 of 12 linked, read 2026-11-05). Its settings have `allowPreferredValuesOnly` on and no preferred values, so whether a designer can insert `_Segmented control item` from the UI is to be checked: the Item may need to be its preferred value (call 7 expects designers to add Items).

## Axes

`Segmented control` (the set):

| Axis | Values (default first) | Drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Size` | Medium · Small · Large (call 3; Large added last, V5, first on the grid) | Container min height 48 · 36 · 56 and padding 6 · 4 · 4, the Root's Hit target |
| `State` | Enabled · Disabled · Skeleton (call 8 reversed) | track fill; Disabled is the root at opacity 0.5 over the Enabled look (U2); Skeleton hides `Segments` |
| `Floating` | False · True (R2) | Skeleton and the Items are unchanged |

Properties: SLOT `Segments`, added by hand. 3 × 3 × 2 × 2 = **36 variants**. `Floating` sits before `iOS`, since `iOS` is always last (ADR 0005); every instance placed is `Floating=False`.

`_Segmented control item` (private):

| Axis | Values (default first) | Drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Selected` | False · True | the indicator fill and the label colour |
| `State` | Enabled · Pressed · Focus · Disabled · Hover (W8) | the press and hover fills, the Focus stroke, the disabled colours |
| `Size` | Medium · Small · Large | side padding and min height; the label is `Body/Subheadline` on Medium and Small, `Body/Body` on Large (V4) |

Properties: TEXT `Label`. 2 × 5 × 3 × 2 = **60 variants**. The set pins its three default Items' `Size` and `iOS` to its own. A designer sets both on each Item they add, since Slot content isn't pinned (call 7, R1).

- There is no Error or Loading state: a segmented control picks between values that are always valid.
- There are no booleans. The number of segments is the number of Items in the Slot (call 1).

None. The segments are text only (call 2), so there is no `Segmented control/Icon/*` record.

## Text styles, wrapping and truncation

| Layer | Text style (Medium · Small) | Lines | Text property |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Label` (Item) | `Body/Subheadline` · `Body/Subheadline`, Type Emphasis Regular unselected | one line at standard sizes, up to two at AX sizes, never truncated (call 16). In Figma Fill × Hug, `HEIGHT`, centred, with no `maxLines` (T1) | `Label` |

- The kit truncates `ENDING`. Ours doesn't: copy that doesn't fit at 375 is a copy problem, or a sign that Tabs fit better (call 9). At AX sizes, a label that would need a third line switches the control to a Select (T3, T4).
- `Body/Subheadline` matches the Toolbars' part and Button's Small and Medium labels ([button.md](button.md)).

### Nine period labels at 375 (the AC's check)

The widths were shaped with HarfBuzz on Finnomena Trek 1.002 OTFs, with SF's tracking, on 2026-11-04. They are rounded to 0.1 px. The available width is 375 − 2 × 16 margin = 343. One segment is (343 − 2 × 2 track padding − 8 × 0 gap) / 9 = **37.7**.

| Labels | Style | Widest | Room per side in 37.7 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `1D 1W 1M 3M 6M YTD 1Y 3Y MAX` | `Body/Subheadline` 15 Regular (Small) | 30.4 (`MAX`) | **3.6: fits** with the Small Item's 2 padding |
| the same | Subheadline 15 Strong (Bold) | 31.6 | 3.0: fits |
| the same | `Body/Body` 17 Regular | 34.0 | 1.8: fits only with 0 padding |
| `1 วัน 1 สัปดาห์ 1 เดือน 3 เดือน 6 เดือน YTD 1 ปี 3 ปี ทั้งหมด` | `Utility/Footnote` 13 Regular | 47.0 (`1 สัปดาห์`) | −4.7: doesn't fit at any size |
| the same | `Body/Subheadline` 15 Regular | 53.3 | −7.8 |

So the nine fit at 375 as Latin abbreviations on `Size=Small`. Spelled-out Thai labels don't fit: they sum to 323 at Subheadline before any padding. Latin abbreviations are within the Thai rules: Caption 2 and up hold Latin abbreviations (Localisation). With the kit's gap 4, a segment drops to 34.1 and the room per side to 1.8, which is why the gap is 0.

## Hit target and Skeleton

- **Hit target**: on the Root, `Interactive/<Size>/Interactive min-height` (48 · 48 · 56), with Container centred (ADR 0003). The Small track (36) overflows to 48 tall.
- **Segment width under 48**: each segment is its own target, and its width is the track's share. Five segments at 343 are 67.8 wide. Nine are 37.7, under 48 but over WCAG 2.5.8's 24 (call 10). Segments touch, so no target overlaps another.
- **Skeleton**: `State=Skeleton`, the same in both looks. The Container is filled `Skeleton/skeleton-background` at its min height, with `Segments` hidden.

## Motion

Motion and haptics, the Segmented control row, made specific:

| Transition | Token | Reduced form | Haptic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The selected indicator slides to the tapped segment, and the two labels swap colour with it | `moderate-01 · standard` (iOS `.smooth`) | `fast-02 · standard` crossfade of the indicator and the labels | `Selection`, once, when the selection moves (not on a tap of the selected segment) |
| Pressed fill on an unselected segment | `fast-01 · standard` | kept (colour only) | None |
| Focus stroke | `fast-01 · standard` | kept | None |
| Skeleton | the Skeleton's own | static shape, no shimmer | None |

- In Figma the indicator is each Item's `Selected` fill, and nothing moves (call 12).
- on `iOS=False` the outline does. Nothing else moves.
- Hover (`State=Hover`) fades in at `fast-01 · standard`, as Pressed; kept under Reduced motion, no haptic.

## Usage

- Both looks side by side, Light and Dark, at Medium, with Thai copy `ทั้งหมด · กองทุน · หุ้น`.
- The Amount entry case, `บาท · หน่วย`, above an Amount field (Amount entry). Amount field isn't built, so the sample uses Text field with `Unit` on (` ฿`), its Thai title and helper set by override; swap it once Amount field exists.
- The period selector: `Size=Small`, nine Latin labels `1D … MAX`, at 375 wide, with `1Y` selected.
- The Focus ring once, on `iOS=False`.
- A `Floating=True` Medium control, 280 wide, `ภาพรวม · ปรับพอร์ต` (R2), centred 16 above the bottom of a 375 × 296 screen over a scrolled list of `List item` rows.

Use it to switch between two to five views or filters of the same content, where every option fits on one line at standard text sizes. Two closely related views of one object, such as a plan's Overview and Rebalance, take a Segmented control, `Floating=True` when it sits over scrolling content. Separate sections of the app are Navigation. It isn't for navigation between sections (that's Tabs), for a choice inside a form with many options (Select or Radio), or for an on/off (Toggle).
