# Menu

> Core's Menu, compiled for the web: the `iOS=False` look at the Large text size. Generated by the Core sync; do not edit.

## Anatomy and layer tree

Since the variant root is the trigger's 48 × 48, and the open menu is drawn past it from the anchored corner:

```
Menu (variant)                         Fixed 48 × 48, no fill, clip off, no auto-layout
├─ Trigger                             Expand=False only; exposed instance, x 0 y 0
└─ Menu                                Expand=True only; 238 × Hug, top 0; Top right: x −190, constraints Right × Top; Top left: x 0, Left × Top
```

`Menu` holds the kit's tree, kept for both looks; changes are in brackets. As it was on 2026-11-07 in the kit (its root, now the `Menu` frame):

```
Menu                                   238 × Hug, V, padding 10/0/10/0, gap 0 [`Menu/Container/Default/Layout width`, `Padding top|bottom`]
├─ Surface                             absolute, Stretch × Stretch
│                                        iOS=False: a frame, `Layer/layer-01`, radius 12 (`radius-md`, M14), the Floating shadow (M15)
├─ Control Group                       Fill × Hug, H, padding 0/10, gap 6 [BOOLEAN `Show Control Group`, M9; `Menu/Control group/Default/*`]
│  └─ Action 1–3  <_Menu Quick Action> Fill × Hug [min height `Menu/Quick action/Default/Layout height` 56; built 59]
└─ Menu Items                          SLOT, Fill × Hug, V, padding 0/16/0/16 [sides `Menu/Container/Default/Padding left|right`]
   ├─ <_Menu Items>                    Fill × Hug
   ├─ <_Menu Items - Submenus>         Fill × Hug
   ├─ <_Menu Separator>                Fill × Hug
   └─ <_Menu Section Title>            Fill × Hug
```

The parts are the same in both looks. Their names are M10:

```
_Menu Items (variant)                  Fixed 190 × Hug, H, padding 0/8/0/6, gap 6, radius-round [`Menu/Item/Default/*`], min height 48 [Interactive/Medium] (kit 40)
├─ Frame                               24 × 24: Checkmark (`Menu has Selection=True` only)
├─ Leading                             Fill × Hug, H, gap 8 [`Menu/Leading/Default/Gap horizontal`]
│  ├─ Symbol                           28 wide, fixed (glyphs swap)
│  └─ Label and Subtitle               Fill × Hug, V, padding 12/0/12/0 [`Menu/Item/Default/Padding top|bottom`] (kit 10), gap 2 [`Menu/Label/Default/Gap vertical`]
│     ├─ Label                         wraps
│     └─ Subtitle                      `Show Subtitle` (off)
└─ Shortcuts                           [dropped, M8]

_Menu Items - Submenus (variant)       as `_Menu Items`, with a trailing chevron in place of Shortcuts;
                                       `Menu has Selection=True`: no checkmark, padding left 36 [`Menu/Submenu/Selection/Padding left`], in line with checkmark rows;
                                       `Open=True`: the pill, `layer-selected-02` fill, chevron down
_Menu Profile Item (variant)           as `_Menu Items`, an exposed `Avatar` `Size=Small` (36) in place of Symbol, centred;
_Menu Section Title                    Fixed × Hug, H, padding 4/8/8/8 [`Menu/Section title/Default/*`], Header text: 32 tall (owner, 2026-11-08)
_Menu Separator                        Fixed × Hug, V, padding 10/8/10/8 [`Menu/Separator/Default/*`], a 1-tall line
_Menu Quick Action (variant)           Fixed × Hug, V, padding 6/4/6/4, gap 5, radius 20 [`Menu/Quick action/Default/*`]; BG (absolute, `Show Selection`), Symbol, Label
```

On `State=Hover` and `State=Pressed` (M16), an item's root takes a `radius-round` pill fill, the same shape as an open submenu. On `State=Focus` the same pill takes a 2 `Focus/focus` stroke, centred and out of layout, with no fill: the row stays 48.

Left out of Core (Assumptions): `Edit Menu`, `_Edit Menu Item`, `_Menu Indicator` (the Edit Menu's more button), `Text Selection`, `_Selection`.

## Hit target and Skeleton

- **Hit target**: each item row is a target. `_Menu Items` and `_Menu Items - Submenus` bind the min height on their row (the variant root) to `Interactive/Medium/Interactive min-height` (48), as row components do (ADR 0003, row amendment). `_Menu Quick Action` is 70 × 59 (min height 56), over 48 both ways. `_Menu Profile Item` is 56, its own min height. Section Title and Separator take no tap. This holds for both looks.
- **Skeleton**: none. A menu is an overlay that opens with its content.

## Motion

- iOS opens, morphs and dismisses the menu and plays its own haptics; the button morphs into the menu, which is why `Expand=True` has no button.
- `iOS=False`:
  - Opening: fades and scales from its `Anchor` corner, over the button, `moderate-02 · entrance · scale-in`.
  - Closing: `moderate-01 · exit · scale-in`.
  - Movement from Motion and haptics, the owner's D6 (ads-tokens.md § 5), edited in place at the owner's word ("Edit directly", #811, 2026-11-11).
  - Reduced motion: a crossfade.
  - Haptic: none.
  - Items change state with `fast-01 · standard`, kept under reduced motion.

- [x] 2. Non-text parts: the checkmark and chevrons are `icon-primary` (passes). The hover and pressed fills come with the label and need no 3:1. Separators are decoration.

## Usage

- Page `Menus` (new, between `Lists` and `Password fields`, made with `docs/agents/make-page.js`): `Menu` in Components, the six parts in Items; no Bases.
- Usage samples, in Light and Dark columns (iOS bars' exception). Each look gets a `Screen 402` frame. `Closed`: a toolbar with `Expand=False` in its `Trailing` Slot. `Sort` and `Actions`: `Expand=True · Anchor=Top right` in the button's place, so the menu covers it. The Thai copy:
  - A sort menu: a `เรียงตาม` section with `ชื่อ`, `ราคาล่าสุด` and `ผลตอบแทน 1 ปี`, one of them checked.
  - An actions menu: `แชร์`, `เพิ่มในรายการโปรด`, a separator, then `ลบ` as `Danger`.
  - The `iOS=False` sample shows a hovered row and `เพิ่มในรายการโปรด` at `State=Focus`.
  - A profile list (`iOS=False`,): `สมชาย ใจดี` (checked) and `สมหญิง รักเรียน` on `_Menu Profile Item`, `Menu has Selection=True`, Subtitle off, then a separator and `เพิ่มบัญชี` (glyph `plus`).
  - No result (`iOS=False`,): Core's `Empty state` `Size=Medium` in the Slot, Artwork and Button off: "No results found", "Try another word or check the spelling."
- Notes:
  - that look is for mockups. Android + web use `iOS=False`.
  - **Placement**: a menu **opens from its trigger's corner and covers the trigger** (`iOS=False` covers it too,). `Anchor` Top right for a trigger at the trailing edge, Top left at the leading edge, so it never leaves the screen. A Pattern places it over the screen, never in the Page component (screen-rules). One open at a time.
- A menu holds actions and choices, never a form. A list of more than about 10 rows, or rows with controls, goes in a Sheet (Bottom sheet on Android + web).
