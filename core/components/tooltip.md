# Tooltip

> Core's Tooltip, compiled for the web: the `iOS=False` look at the Large text size. Generated by the Core sync; do not edit.

## Anatomy and layer tree

The tip, `_Tooltip item`, is Carbon's construction with Toggletip's caret and Uber's look:

```
_Tooltip item (variant root)   Hug × Hug, no fill, no padding
└─ Container                   VERTICAL (Top, Bottom) or HORIZONTAL (Left, Right), Hug × Hug, gap 0, no clip;
   │                           cross-axis MIN · CENTER · MAX by Alignment; Caret first (Bottom, Right) or last
   │                           (Top, Left) by itemIndex
   ├─ Caret                    Hug × Hug; padding left and right at Top and Bottom 16 (Medium, Large) or 20 (Small, R7),
   │                           0 at Left and Right
   │  └─ Pointer               _Toggletip caret, Style=Inverse; Direction Down (Top), Up (Bottom), Right (Left),
   │                           Left (Right) (T3); Size Small (8 × 4) for Small, Large (16 × 8) for Medium and Large (R3)
      │                        fill background-inverse, effect Elevation/Floating (on the base)
```

At Start the caret's centre is 24 from the tip's start (16 inset plus half its 16 width), the centre of a 48 Icon button at the trigger's edge, as Toggletip's. Small's caret (Icon's, 8 wide) pads 20, so its centre is 24 in too (R7; Carbon's Icon button tip draws 20, 4 off a 48 button's centre).

The public `Tooltip`, Toggletip's Final set shape (T1):

```
Tooltip (variant root)         Hug × Hug, no fill, no padding: the trigger's size
└─ Container                   VERTICAL (Top, Bottom) or HORIZONTAL (Left, Right), Hug × Hug, gap 0, no clip;
   │                           the overflow first (Top, Left) or last (Bottom, Right)
   ├─ Icon                     Icon button, Tertiary · Default · Small, State Enabled, exposed (its glyph is the
   │                           designer's); Type=Icon
   ├─ Definition               _Toggletip definition, State Enabled, exposed (`Term`); Type=Definition
   └─ Tooltip overflow         0 × 0, no clip; Visible=True only; holds the tip at the trigger's start, centre or
      │                        end (Top, Bottom) or middle (Left, Right)
      └─ Item                  _Tooltip item, Position and Alignment pinned, Size by Type (Icon Small,
```

- The tip meets the trigger's 48 box, 12 from a Small Icon button's glyph (Toggletip's R7), not Uber's 4 from the glyph.
- Definition hugs its term, so at Left and Right the tip would touch it: there the Item's `Caret` pads the pointer 4 (`Spacing/spacing-02`, R5; 8 before) off the term (`paddingRight` at Left, `paddingLeft` at Right, an override on the nested item). The overflow can't carry the gap: Figma widens a 0-wide frame to its padding, so the instance would grow past the term.
- The set clips its own view of open tips; an instance on a screen doesn't (Toggletip's R7).
- No Slots. Nested public instance: Icon button. Private parts: `_Tooltip item`, `_Toggletip definition`, `_Toggletip caret`.

## Axes

`_Tooltip item`:

| Axis | Values (default first) | Drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Position` | Top · Bottom · Left · Right | Container direction, the Caret's index and padding, the Pointer's Direction (T2) |
| `Alignment` | Center · Start · End | Container's cross-axis alignment; Left and Right only at Center (`exclude`) |
| `Size` | Small · Medium · Large | the Surface's padding, the Label's max width, the Pointer's Size (R1; Large,) |

Properties: TEXT `Label`. (4 × 3 − 4 excluded) × 3 = **24 variants**, placed by hand as three compasses (Small, Medium, Large, top to bottom; Build log).

`Tooltip`:

| Axis | Values (default first) | Drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Type` | Icon · Definition | which trigger shows (T1) |
| `Position` | Top · Bottom · Left · Right | as the item's, and where the overflow sits |
| `Alignment` | Center · Start · End | as the item's; Left and Right only at Center |
| `Visible` | False · True | the overflow and its tip |

Properties: none of its own; the exposed Item shows `Label`, the exposed Icon button its glyph, the Definition its `Term`. 2 × 8 × 2 = **32 variants**.

`_Toggletip caret` after T3: `Style` Raised · Inverse × `Direction` Up · Down · Left · Right, Raised × Left and Right excluded: 8 − 2 = **6 variants** (built 2026-11-10; 2 before). (R3) adds `Size` Large · Small, Small Inverse only: 6 + 4 = **10 variants**.

## Hit target and Skeleton

- **Hit target**: the tip is not interactive. The trigger brings its own: the Icon button's 48, the Definition's text box (a term in running text, Toggletip's).
- **Skeleton**: None: an overlay (Variant axes).

## Motion

| Transition | Token | Reduced form | Haptic |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tip opens (fade in) | `fast-02 · entrance` | kept (opacity only) | None |
| Tip closes (fade out) | `fast-01 · exit` | kept | None |

Timing, not motion (as Snackbar's time on screen): it opens after 100 ms of hover and at once on keyboard focus; it closes 300 ms after the pointer leaves both the trigger and the tip, at once on blur or Esc (Carbon's delays). Opening a second tip closes the first. The open token departs from the Motion table's `moderate-02` for a small surface arriving (Assumptions). Motion and haptics' per-component table has its Tooltip row.

## Usage

Samples in Thai, both Theme modes, in the page's `Usage` frame:

- An icon-only button named: a header's search Icon button with `ค้นหากองทุน`, Bottom · End, since the button sits at the screen's right edge.
- A term defined on web: `NAV` with `มูลค่าทรัพย์สินสุทธิต่อหน่วย`, Top · Start, beside its figure.
- Left and Right once each: a `house` button at the left edge (Right, `หน้าหลัก`) and a `gear` button at the right edge (Left, `ตั้งค่า`).
- Don'ts: a link or a button in a tip (use Toggletip); a status or an error (use Snackbar or the field's helper text); a tooltip on iPhone; a tip on a button that already has a visible label, or holding what the user needs to finish the task.

The proposal's fourth sample, a truncated fund name in a table cell, is dropped (owner, 2026-11-10, on #745: "1", drop it rather than add a trigger): the set's triggers are an Icon button and a Definition term, and a cut-off cell is neither. Text cut off by overflow (Uber's second use) has no Tooltip trigger; a table shows the full name its own way.

Where it goes: one at a time, 16 or more from the screen's edge, never cut off; a tip that would leave the screen takes the opposite Position. Content: a few words; no full stop on one sentence, no `?` or `!`; nothing the user needs to finish the task.

The `Icon` trigger is an Icon button pinned Tertiary · Default · Small, as Toggletip's (TT2 in [toggletip.md](toggletip.md)); exposed, so a designer sets the Hierarchy of the control it names. The shared rules: [hierarchy.md](../hierarchy.md).
