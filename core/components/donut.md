# Donut

> Core's Donut, compiled for the web: the `iOS=False` look at the Large text size. Generated by the Core sync; do not edit.

## Anatomy and layer tree

```
Donut (variant root)             vertical auto layout, Hug × Hug, no fill, no padding
└─ Container                     no auto layout, Fixed × Fixed, Layout width and height by Size (120 · 20)
   ├─ Track                      ELLIPSE, full circle, inner radius by Size, fill Chart/chart-empty; Skeleton/skeleton-background on State=Skeleton
   └─ Segments                   SLOT, no auto layout, the root's box; children stacked at 0, 0. Hidden on Empty and Skeleton

_Donut segment                   vertical auto layout, Hug × Hug, no fill
└─ Container                     no auto layout, Fixed × Fixed, Layout width and height by Size (120 · 20)
   └─ Arc                        ELLIPSE, constraints Scale; arcData starts at 12 o'clock (−90°) and runs clockwise; inner radius by Size; fill by Series; 1px Center stroke Layer/layer-01
```

- **A share is an `arcData` override** on each segment's `Arc` (start and end angle), set by a script or by hand in the panel's Arc fields. Every segment starts at 12 o'clock and ends at its running total, the first segment on top (R1, as Carbon), so a designer edits one number per segment. A 1px stroke in the surface colour separates neighbours (R2).
- **The default content** is four segments, 40 · 30 · 20 · 10, `Series` 01, 02, 03 and Other, drawn as running totals 0.4 · 0.7 · 0.9 · 1.0 with Other at the bottom: the allocation bar's default, so the two parts show the same example.
- **No centre text, no legend.** A total in the hole and a legend are the host's (Q1). At Small the hole is 12 wide.
- The `Track` shows wherever the segments don't reach 360° (a total under 100%), and alone on `State=Empty`.

## Axes

| Axis | Values (default first) | Drives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Size` | Large · Small | the box 120 · 20 and the ring 16.8 · 4 (Q1, Q7,) |
| `State` | Data · Empty · Skeleton | Data: segments over the track. Empty: the track alone. Skeleton: the track in the skeleton colour, no segments |

`_Donut segment`: `Series` = 01 · 02 · 03 · 04 · 05 · 06 · 07 · 08 · Other (9) × `Size` Large · Small = **18 variants**. The part takes `Size` because the ring is an inner-radius ratio of the ellipse (0.72 · 0.6), which no scaling of a Large arc gives at Small.

Properties: SLOT `Segments`. 2 × 3 = **6 variants** on Donut. No count axis (Q3).

## Hit target and Skeleton

- **Hit target**: None: static and decorative inside the Today chip, which is the tap target (Button's 48). A tappable slice is out, as the bar's (its Q4); a Large donut that opens a detail sits inside a tappable host.
- **Skeleton**: `State=Skeleton`: the track in `skeleton-background`, no segments, the same box as Data so nothing shifts. Inside the chip, Button's own Skeleton hides its content, so the chip never shows this state.

## Usage

- Both modes, Light and its Dark twin, with Thai copy around it (Localisation).
- **The Today chip** (Overview): `Size=Small`, one segment per asset class (สินทรัพย์หลัก), each Arc's fill overridden to `Chart/Asset allocation/<member>` (equity, fixed-income, gold, cash, government-bond, miscellaneous). It can't sit in Button's leading place until Button takes leading artwork (Q2); until then the chip keeps `chart-pie` (#803's Out of scope).
- **Current vs new** (Rebalance compare): two `Size=Large` donuts under the column labels สัดส่วนปัจจุบัน | สัดส่วนที่แนะนำ (rebalance-compare.md, DES-13 Q15), one segment per fund. The same fund takes the same `Series` in both, so a fund keeps its colour from current to new (Q5, Q6); a fund at 0% keeps no segment.
- Use it for a whole split into 2–9 parts at a glance, beside labels or as a decoration of a labelled control. Fold a ninth and later part into `Other`. Don't use it to compare parts precisely (a bar or rows read lengths better), for a trend, or for one amount.
- Set each share by the segment's Arc end angle, from 12 o'clock clockwise: every Arc starts at `−90°` and ends at `−90° + 360·T`, where `T` (0–1) is its share plus every share before it; the first segment sits on top, the last at the bottom.
