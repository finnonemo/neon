# Popover

> Core's Popover, compiled for the web: the `iOS=False` look at the Large text size. Generated by the Core sync; do not edit.

## Anatomy and layer tree

The kit's variant is a flat frame of three absolute layers. Ours keeps its two looks in one tree and adds the Slot the kit lacks (P5), as Sheet adds its Content:

```
Popovers (iPad Only) (variant root)  Hug × Hug, no fill, no padding; the Arrow overflows it, as the kit's does (P2)
│  │                                   iOS=False: radius 12 (`radius-md`); `Layer/layer-01`, 1 `border-subtle` inside, `Elevation/Floating`
│  └─ Content                        SLOT, Fill × Hug, VERTICAL, padding 16 (P6) [new: the kit has none]
                                       iOS=False: `Seam` (56 × 1, over the Surface's border at the base), `Arrow shape` (filled `layer-01`) and `Arrow border` (the curve as an open 1 `border-subtle` path)
```

- **Edge** moves the Arrow to one of the four sides and one of three places on it, as the kit does: Center on the side's middle, Leading and Trailing 28 either way (the kit's offset at 200; it is read again on the 352 surface at build, assumption A2). The `Edge` words keep the kit's: the arrow sits on that edge of the popover, so `Bottom (…)` opens above its anchor, the reverse of Toggletip's and Carbon's `Position`.
- structure.md item 12 bans a VECTOR in a variant and only the Kit parts keep theirs; the Popover isn't a Kit part, so this is P4(b), the exception the owner asked for.
- **Content** is a SLOT (figma-instances.md) with no default text: a Popover holds a view (a form, a filter, a short list) that a Pattern supplies. The Usage samples fill it with a Thai filter form.
- **No anchor in the set**, as Toggletip's Q3: the trigger belongs to the Pattern.

## Component variables and Theme tokens

`Components`, in Tag's three-level shape; the radius differs by look, so `iOS` is a path segment (Variant axes, structure.md). Nothing else varies, so the other groups are keyed `Default`:

| Group | Keyed by | Properties | Values |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Popover/Surface/Default` | none varies | Layout width | 352 (raw, P5) |
| `Popover/Surface/True` | `iOS` | Radius | 38 (raw: the kit's `Popover Corner Radius`; A4) |
| `Popover/Surface/False` | `iOS` | Radius, Stroke weight | 12 (`Radius/12px (radius-md) = 12`), 1 (raw) |
| `Popover/Content/Default` | none varies | Padding left / right / top / bottom | 16 (`Spacing/spacing-05 = 16`, P6) |

The Arrow's size is the kit's (raw). Core's `iOS UI Kit` collection holds `Sheet/*` radii but no popover one; A4 asks whether to add one.

No new Theme token. `iOS=False` binds the ones Toggletip and Menu bind (the Arrow too);

| Theme token | Light | Dark | Paired with |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Layer/layer-01` (Surface fill, `iOS=False`) | `White/100` | `Grey/90` | `Background/background-primary` (the page behind) |
| `Border/border-subtle` (Surface stroke, `iOS=False`) | `Black/12A` | `White/18A` | `Layer/layer-01` |

Contrast (`contrast.mjs`, 2026-11-11): `border-subtle` on `layer-01` 1.32 / 1.76 (Light / Dark); `layer-01` on `background-primary` 1.00 / 1.21. The surface's edge is drawn by the shadow and the border, not by contrast, as Toggletip's and a Tile's are (Visuals). The Popover draws no text of its own; the Slot's content brings its pairs on `layer-01`. The spec's `contrast` list does not apply (Spec: None).

## Icon records

None. The Arrow is a shape, not a glyph; the Slot's content brings its icons (Font Awesome).

## Text styles, wrapping and truncation

None of its own: the Popover draws no text. The Slot's content brings its text styles; it wraps inside the 352 width (Thai floors 12 on one line, 13 where it wraps, Localisation).

## Hit target and Skeleton

- **Hit target**: none. The Popover isn't a control: its anchor and the controls in its Slot bring their own. A tap outside closes it, so the tap target is the page (the app's), not a layer here.
- **Skeleton**: None. An iOS component and an overlay (Variant axes); the view in its Slot brings its own.

## Accessibility

- [x] 1. Text pairs: the Popover draws none; The Usage samples (Light and Dark) put Radio, Select, Link and Button in it; their pairs are those components' own.
- [ ] 2. Non-text parts: the surface's edge is drawn by shadow and border (1.32 / 1.76), decorative, not meaningful: the Popover's boundary is also set by the content inside it and by the shadow. If the owner wants the 3:1 edge, `border-primary` is the token (P8).
- [x] 3. N/A: not a field.
- [x] 4. Nothing is shown by colour, motion or a haptic alone: the popover's presence is announced (item 9).
- [x] 5. N/A: not focusable as a control; focus rings belong to the controls in the Slot.
- [x] 6. A Select in the Slot reads as overflowing at every mode, Large included: the check places the main's Fixed 343, where the Usage samples set it Fill. Past about half the screen the content scrolls (the Pattern's call, as Toggletip's).
- [x] 7. Smallest text: none of its own; the Slot's floor is the Thai 12 / 13.
- [x] 8. The Motion part names each transition's token, Reduced form and haptic.
- [ ] 9. Role, name, states and focus order (to confirm with engineering):

|  | Web |
| --- | --- |

## Usage

The page's `Usage` frame (structure.md) shows, in Light and Dark, each look with the same Thai view in its Slot ("ตัวกรองกองทุน": a Radio group "ประเภทกองทุน", a Select "ระดับความเสี่ยง", a "ล้างค่า" Link and a Primary "ใช้ตัวกรอง" Button):

- **Where it sits.** Width picks the component (ADR 0013, Regular width): at iPad's regular width and on web desktop, a Popover anchored to its control; The Usage shows the iPhone case as that Sheet with the same Slot content, as the issue asks.
- **Edge samples**: the three Top and Bottom Centers, `Leading (Top)` and `Trailing (Top)`, at both looks, so a designer sees where the arrow lands; the rest are in the set. `iOS=False` repeats one surface and its Arrow (P2).
- **Placement**: the Popover's edge sits 8 from its anchor (`spacing-03`, P7), hand-placed; at both looks the arrow's tip is the kit's 13, so the tip sits 8 from the anchor and the surface 21.
- **Dismiss**: tap outside or Esc; a Primary or Secondary Button in the Slot ("Got it", "ใช้ตัวกรอง") for a view that needs one, as Base's Popover asks. No ✕ is built in (P9).
- **Not for**: an explanation of its anchor (a [Toggletip](toggletip.md) or a [Tooltip](tooltip.md)); a list of actions (a [Menu](menu.md)); a decision that stops the user (an [Alert](alert.md)); more than one screen of content or a flow (a Sheet at every width).
- **Hierarchy**: N/A: it nests no button. A Button in the Slot is the Pattern's; Base gives its dismiss Primary or Secondary ([hierarchy.md](../hierarchy.md)).
- **Focus ring**: not drawn: the Popover isn't interactive.
