# Immersive pages keep a thin Brand Floor

Narrowed by [ADR 0005](0005-immersive-pages-start-from-a-guideline-theme.md): this now holds for Reference-led pages only.

On immersive pages, Finnomena's identity may shrink to its logo, IBM Plex Sans Thai and at least one identifiable Finnomena color; navy and yellow are not required, and an action may be white or another accessible color. At Reference-led, a reference's accents and action colors may survive alongside that one Finnomena color. Campaign and event pages need room for themes such as a dark racing dashboard, where a mandatory navy or yellow action fights the story; the type and a color are enough to read as Finnomena, while the legal limit (no source-brand logo, imagery, copy, product name or font file) and accessible contrast stay fixed. Functional screens, the shared navy primary mapping and semantic status colors are unchanged, and kept colors stay page-scoped.

## Considered Options

- **Finnomena accent and action colors at every level**: rejected; it forced navy or yellow actions onto pages whose theme they contradict, and employees read Finnomena as only navy and yellow.
- **Typeface only, no Finnomena color**: rejected; with the logo as the only other mark, a Reference-led page could become indistinguishable from the reference's own brand.
