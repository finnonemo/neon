# Employees bring Reference Designs; Neon ships none

Neon does not bundle third-party DESIGN.md files. Employees bring their own Reference Design (for example from Refero Styles), and the Brand Floor strips anything that would carry the source brand into a Finnomena page. Bundling styles extracted from other companies' sites would make Neon distribute unlicensed brand material and invite pages that impersonate those brands; the only reference designs Neon may later ship are Finnomena-made examples reviewed through the example library.
